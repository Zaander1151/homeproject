## Shopping Cart Checklist

### ✅ Soldering Equipment - $90

- [ ] **Soldering Iron** | $35-40
  - **RECOMMENDED:** Pine64 Pinecil V2 (USB-C powered)
  - **Search:** "Pinecil V2 soldering iron" on Amazon.ca
  - **Alternative:** Hakko FX-888D ($100-120) - search "Hakko FX-888D"
  - **Must have:** Temperature control, 60W+ power
  - **Verify:** Includes power adapter OR use your 65W USB-C laptop charger

- [ ] **Solder** | $12-15
  - **Search:** "60/40 rosin core solder 0.8mm" or "Kester solder"
  - **Size:** 0.8mm or 1.0mm diameter
  - **Amount:** 100g spool minimum
  - **Type:** 60/40 tin-lead (easier for beginners) OR lead-free if preferred

- [ ] **Helping Hands with Magnifier** | $15
  - **Search:** "helping hands soldering" or "third hand tool magnifier"
  - **Must have:** 2+ adjustable arms with alligator clips, magnifying glass

- [ ] **Solder Sucker + Desoldering Wick** | $10
  - **Search:** "solder sucker desoldering pump wick kit"
  - **Often sold together** as a kit

- [ ] **Brass Wire Tip Cleaner** | $8
  - **Search:** "brass wire solder tip cleaner" or "brass sponge soldering"
  - **Check:** Often included with soldering iron stand

- [ ] **Tip Tinner/Cleaner** | $10
  - **Search:** "solder tip tinner" or "Thermaltronics tip tinner"
  - **Size:** 15g tin is sufficient

**Soldering Subtotal:** ~$90

## Quality Check Before Ordering

### Verify These Product Features

**Soldering Iron:**
- [ ] Temperature adjustable (200-450°C range minimum)
- [ ] 60W power minimum
- [ ] Includes stand OR buy separate stand
- [ ] Includes tips OR tips available to purchase separately

**Multimeter:**
- [ ] Auto-ranging (says "Auto" on product)
- [ ] Continuity test with beeper
- [ ] DC voltage: 0-600V minimum
- [ ] Resistance: 0-20MΩ minimum
- [ ] Includes: Test leads, battery, user manual

**ESD Mat:**
- [ ] Size at least 24" x 16" (bigger is better)
- [ ] Includes grounding snap/stud
- [ ] Includes wrist strap with ground cord
- [ ] Two-layer construction (top dissipative, bottom conductive)

**Wire Strippers:**
- [ ] Self-adjusting mechanism (one squeeze, auto-strips)
- [ ] Range includes 18-22 AWG (most common for ESP32 work)
- [ ] Comfortable grip for long sessions

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### "Pinecil not available on Amazon.ca"
- **Solution 1:** Order direct from Pine64 store (pine64.com) - shipping to Canada ~$10-15 USD
- **Solution 2:** Buy Hakko FX-888D instead (more expensive but widely available)
- **Solution 3:** Search "TS100 soldering iron" - similar portable option

### "Klein multimeter is $60+ CAD"
- **Solution:** Buy AstroAI DT132A for $22-28 instead - excellent beginner multimeter
- **Difference:** Klein is more durable, louder beeper, better warranty
- **Reality:** AstroAI works great for home electronics

### "ESD mat + wrist strap is $40-50"
- **Solution:** Search "anti-static mat 24x16" without brand names
- **Alternative:** Buy wrist strap separately ($8) + DIY mat (aluminum foil + fabric, $0)
- **Safety note:** Proper ESD mat is highly recommended for ESP32 work

### "Total is $280-300, not $250"
- **Cause:** Canadian prices fluctuate, some items may have increased
- **Solution:** Use budget optimization tips above
- **Priority order:**
  1. Soldering iron + solder (can't do anything without this)
  2. Multimeter (essential for debugging)
  3. Wire cutters + strippers (constant use)
  4. ESD mat (protect your ESP32s)
  5. Other tools (nice to have, can buy later)
