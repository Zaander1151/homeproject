### Soldering Iron Maintenance

**Every Session:**
1. **Before use:** Turn on, let heat to 350°C
2. **Tin the tip:** Apply fresh solder to coat tip
3. **During use:** Wipe on brass wool between joints
4. **After use:** Tin tip before turning off (protects from oxidation)

**Weekly:**
1. **Clean thoroughly:** Brass wool + tip tinner
2. **Inspect tip:** Look for pitting or damage
3. **Check temperature:** Verify with temperature tester (optional)

**Monthly:**
1. **Deep clean:** Use tip tinner/cleaner paste
2. **Inspect heating element:** Check for loose connections
3. **Clean base:** Wipe down soldering station

**Signs You Need a New Tip:**
- Black oxidation won't clean off
- Pitting or holes in tip
- Solder won't stick (even after cleaning)
- Takes too long to heat
- **Replacement cost:** $5-15 depending on type

---

### Bench Power Supply Care

**Daily:**
- Turn off when not in use (don't leave on overnight)
- Verify output voltage is 0V before disconnecting

**Weekly:**
- Wipe down with dry cloth
- Check output cables for damage

**Monthly:**
- Test current limiting function
- Verify voltage accuracy with multimeter
- Clean ventilation holes (don't block airflow)

**Never:**
- Exceed 10A output (can damage unit)
- Short circuit outputs intentionally
- Block ventilation (can overheat)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Workbench Setup

**Q: Can I use a smaller desk than 6 feet?**
A: Yes! 4 feet is the minimum functional size. You'll have less space for simultaneous projects, but it's still workable. Consider adding wall-mounted pegboard or shelving for extra storage.

**Q: Do I really need an ESD mat?**
A: **YES.** ESP32 chips are static-sensitive. You have $50+ worth of boards - a $25 ESD mat is cheap insurance. Static electricity can damage microcontrollers invisibly (works at first, fails later).

**Q: Is the Gridfinity system worth it?**

### Tools & Equipment

**Q: Can I use a cheaper soldering iron than the Weller?**
A: You already have the Weller - keep it! It's excellent. For others: Temperature control is CRITICAL. Cheap $15 irons overheat and damage components. Minimum: $50 temperature-controlled station.

**Q: Do I need an oscilloscope?**
A: Not yet. Oscilloscopes are for advanced debugging (waveforms, timing, analog signals). Focus on digital projects first. Logic analyzer ($15) is more useful for ESP32 I2C/SPI debugging.

**Q: What's the difference between the bench power supply and USB power?**
A:
- **USB power:** Fixed 5V, convenient for ESP32
- **Bench supply:** Variable 0-30V, current limiting, test at different voltages, power sensors/relays that need 12V

Your Nawei NP3010 is more versatile and essential for serious projects.

- **Lead-free:** Harder, requires 200-230°C, more environmentally friendly
- **Recommendation:** Learn with leaded, wash hands after. Switch to lead-free once comfortable.

### Safety

**Q: How dangerous is soldering?**
A: Moderately safe if you follow precautions:
- **Burns:** Hot iron (350°C) - use stand, don't touch tip
- **Fumes:** Rosin flux irritates lungs - use fume extractor
- **Lead:** Wash hands after using leaded solder
- **Fire:** Rare but possible - keep fire extinguisher nearby

You have fume extractor and safety glasses - you're doing well! Add fire extinguisher.

**Q: Can I solder in my bedroom?**
A: Not recommended. Solder fumes are irritating even with extractor. Ideal locations:
- Garage
- Basement workshop
- Spare room with window
- Well-ventilated office

If bedroom is only option: Window open, fume extractor running, door open for cross-ventilation.
